# BiBroker Personas (Draft)

## 1. Roger Binder – Founder / Power User
- **Goals:** Daily situational awareness of holdings, quick rebalance intuition, easy sharing with partners/investors.
- **Pain points:** Manual spreadsheet upkeep, ticker mismatches between exchanges, lack of narrative summaries.
- **Needs:** Morning digest, deep-dive workspace, fast export to PDF/DOCX, conversational Q&A interface.

## 2. Advisor / Analyst (TBD)
- **Goals:** Review Roger’s positions, propose actions, validate diversification.
- **Pain points:** Access to up-to-date data, reconciling watchlists, generating professional-looking briefs.
- **Needs:** Shared workspace view, comment threads/annotations, ability to highlight risks/opportunities.

## 3. Finance Ops / Bookkeeper (Future)
- **Goals:** Reconcile brokerage statements, ensure accurate cost basis for accounting.
- **Pain points:** Parsing PDFs, currency conversions, tax lot tracking.
- **Needs:** Reliable ingestion pipeline, audit trail, export to accounting systems.

> These personas are placeholders until stakeholder interviews confirm roles/responsibilities.

## Journey Snapshot (Roger)
1. Upload or sync latest holdings data.
2. Review the “allocation health” dashboard.
3. Receive automated insights/recommendations.
4. Export briefing to share or archive.
5. Ask follow-up questions via co-pilot chat/API.

Document discoveries or adjustments here as we learn more.
